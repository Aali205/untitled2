Our Story

Where Damascene hospitality meets the patience of the chocolatier.

Chocola Teno was born in Damascus with a simple belief: a piece of chocolate is a gesture. Every truffle is rolled, enrobed and wrapped by hand, then dressed in satin ribbon and gold — because in our culture, how you give matters as much as what you give.

Today our boutiques carry that spirit from the streets of Al-Maliki and Mezzeh to Lattakia, Muscat and the Netherlands — the same recipes, the same care, the same smile at the door.
